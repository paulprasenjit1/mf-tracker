Before we start — please read this

What this app is: an educational tool that reads your mutual fund statement, shows where your money sits, and points out things worth understanding — like duplicate funds, high concentration, or a mix that may not suit your age and goals.

What this app is NOT: it is not investment advice and it is not from a SEBI-registered investment adviser. It uses simple, transparent rules. Before you buy or sell anything, please talk to a SEBI-registered adviser or your distributor.

Your privacy: everything you enter stays on this phone. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

Your responsibility: screenshot reading can make mistakes. Always check the numbers on the review screen. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks — read all scheme-related documents carefully.

📖 What do these words mean?

NAVs are official AMFI values (via mfapi.in with AMFI fallback), fetched live in your browser; if stale, the fund is flagged and the last good value is shown. All data stays on this phone. The On-track/Watch/Review notes come from a transparent rules engine using your age, horizon and risk answers — they are educational observations, not investment advice, and not from a SEBI-registered adviser.
